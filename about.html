Director · Producer · Los Angeles

New stories, brought to life with new tools.

Quinn Halleck is a director and producer at the front edge of AI in film and television. His mission is simple: get the stories that never make it through the system made, and made well, using the newest tools to build worlds at a speed and scale that used to take a studio.

He came up the traditional way, mentored by Michael Bay and producing alongside Mark Wahlberg, DJ Khaled and Jeremy Irons, then rebuilt that playbook around AI. At Fox Entertainment he produced The Masked Singer and led the studio's first AI work in live production. For TMZ he produced Celebrity Crime Scene, an AI-recreated true-crime pilot that hit Hulu's Top 10 twice. His short Sigma_001 took him to Cannes to speak on AI in film; his horror short From Where It Hides is distributed by ALTER. Today he runs Moire Media, an AI-native studio in Los Angeles, where everything on this site is made.
